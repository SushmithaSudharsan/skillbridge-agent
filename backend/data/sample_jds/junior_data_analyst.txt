# title: Junior Data Analyst
# role: Data Analyst
# level: fresher
Junior Data Analyst - Lumen Retail Group (fictional)

About the role
You will turn store and online sales data into weekly reports that our merchandising team uses to plan stock.

What you will do
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI
- Write SQL to pull and join sales, stock and returns data
- Explain trends and anomalies to non-technical managers

Must have
- Good SQL (joins, group by, window functions a plus)
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, lookups)
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Clear communication with business stakeholders

Nice to have
- Python with Pandas
- Basic statistics

Experience: 0-1 years; internships count.
