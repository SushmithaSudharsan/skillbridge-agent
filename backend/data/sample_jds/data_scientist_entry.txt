Data Scientist (Entry Level) - Orbit Fintech (fictional)

We are looking for an entry-level data scientist to join our risk team.

Responsibilities
- Build and evaluate classification models for credit risk and churn
- Design and analyse A/B tests for product changes
- Present findings to product managers

Requirements
- Strong Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn)
- Solid foundation in statistics and hypothesis testing
- Experience with SQL
- Hands-on experience with A/B testing
- Ability to explain model results to non-technical stakeholders

Preferred
- Experience with gradient boosting (XGBoost or LightGBM)
- Internship experience is preferred.

0-2 years of experience, including internships.
